What Goes in the Registry

Three jurisdictions. Primary sources only.

🇺🇸
United States

DOJ press releases, HUD records, court filings, GAO reports, congressional appropriation records.

🇵🇷
Puerto Rico

FEMA disaster records, HUD-CDBG-DR filings, OCFO documents, court records, territorial government data.

🇵🇭
Philippines

COA audit reports, DSWD records, ODA project data, Commission on Audit filings, court records.

Every entry in the registry is extracted from one of these primary source types, reviewed editorially before publication, and archived independently of any institutional or advertiser relationship. Analysis and editorial interpretation are clearly distinguished from documented fact.
