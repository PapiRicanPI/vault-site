Research Access

Verified access for journalists and researchers.

The Vault works with journalists and researchers whose investigations intersect with our registry scope — under terms that protect sources, archive integrity, and the operational security of ongoing investigations.

How to Apply

Applications are open by request.

Application Portal — Restoring

The structured application portal is currently being restored and will be online shortly. In the meantime, applications are processed directly by email. Response time is within 72 hours.

To begin the vetting process, send the following to the address below:

  • Your name — or the name you wish to use for correspondence
  • Affiliation — publication, institution, or a statement of independent status
  • Research interest — a brief description of what you are working on and what you are looking for
  • Preferred contact method — how you want to receive a response

Collaboration Terms

Who we work with and how.

Journalists

If your reporting intersects with The Vault's jurisdictions and subject domains, we may be able to provide verified primary-source material, case file access, or research assistance under agreed terms. We are a potential reporting partner, not a source agency.

Researchers

Academic researchers and independent analysts working on poverty-relief systems, NGO accountability, disaster recovery policy, or related fields may apply for archive access. Applications are evaluated on research purpose, affiliation, and relevance to our active files.

All collaboration operates under terms that protect The Vault's sources, ongoing investigations, and archive integrity. Background and credentials may be requested as part of the vetting process.
