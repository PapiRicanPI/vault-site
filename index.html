What we do

We build the record before we build the story.

The Vault is an independent public-interest investigative and archival project examining how poverty, vulnerability, charitable activity, influence and digital media can be converted into profit, power, reputation and control.

01 / PRESERVE

Evidence preservation

Public records, source documents, archived media, platform activity, timelines and supporting material are organized for review.

02 / FOLLOW

Follow the pathways

We examine financial flows, organizational relationships, payment systems, public claims, platform incentives and regulatory gaps.

03 / VERIFY

Claim discipline

Documented fact is separated from interpretation, inference and unresolved questions.

04 / REPORT

Dignity-centered journalism

We investigate exploitation without recreating humiliation, voyeurism or unnecessary exposure.

Our evidence standard

Every conclusion should reveal its evidentiary weight.

Clear labeling protects the record, the subjects of reporting and the reader.

Documented fact

Supported by verifiable records, direct evidence or reliable primary sources.

Interpretation

A reasoned explanation based on the available evidence and context.

Inference

A plausible conclusion that has not yet been directly established.

Unresolved question

A material issue requiring additional evidence or investigation.
