What we do

We build the record before we build the story.

The Vault is a public-interest investigative and archival project. Our work begins with documents, context, and verifiable source material—not outrage, rumor, or spectacle.

01 / PRESERVE

Evidence preservation

We collect and organize public records, platform activity, source documents, archived media, timelines, and supporting material.

02 / ANALYZE

Investigative analysis

We map relationships, financial pathways, organizational structures, regulatory failures, and repeatable patterns of exploitation.

03 / VERIFY

Claim discipline

We identify what is documented, what is interpreted, what remains inferential, and what still requires evidence.

04 / REPORT

Dignity-centered reporting

We report on systems and conduct without reproducing the humiliation, exposure, or exploitation under investigation.

What we are not

Accountability is not vigilantism.

The Vault does not substitute accusation for proof. We do not expose people merely because information is available, and we do not turn vulnerable communities into material for attention.

Not a gossip forum

Private information, source targeting, threats, and coordinated abuse are prohibited.

×
Not a public evidence dump

×
Not a shortcut around due process

Public anger does not replace verification, context, legal process, or editorial restraint.

Not another poverty-content machine

Children, families, survivors, and struggling communities are not props for engagement.

Our investigative standard

Every conclusion must show its evidentiary weight.

This distinction protects the integrity of the investigation, reduces harm, and makes clear where the record is strong—and where it is not yet complete.

Documented fact

Supported by verifiable records, direct evidence, or reliable primary sources.

Interpretation

A reasoned explanation drawn from the available evidence and context.

Inference

A plausible conclusion that has not yet been directly established.

Unresolved question

A material issue that requires additional evidence or investigation.

Dignity is part of the standard

Investigating exploitation without reproducing it.

The people appearing in poverty-related content are human beings with rights, histories, families, and futures. They are not thumbnails, case numbers, or instruments for someone else’s reputation.

That means protecting identities when appropriate, limiting unnecessary exposure, preserving context, respecting informed participation, and refusing to convert suffering into spectacle.

Vetted research environment

Access follows responsibility.

Approved members receive only the permissions required for their assigned role. A role is not a status symbol. It is a defined level of responsibility, trust, and system access.

ADMIN

System administration, access governance, approvals, configuration, and incident authority.

CUSTODIAN

Evidence management, archival control, workspace oversight, and records integrity.

RESEARCHER

Authorized investigative research, source development, analysis, and case collaboration.

OBSERVER

Restricted read-only access to specifically approved materials and workspaces.
